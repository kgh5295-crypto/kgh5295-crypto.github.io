SOLMARA

Nestled in the spirit of the Mediterranean, Solmara brings together coastal flavors, thoughtfully crafted dishes, and timeless hospitality.

Inspired by the warmth of the sea and the beauty of European dining, every detail is designed to make each visit an experience worth savoring.

Fall Favorites

Herb-Roasted Chicken

Tender herb-roasted chicken served over seasoned quinoa with roasted seasonal vegetables, finished with a creamy roasted pepper sauce and fresh microgreens.

Spiced Pumpkin Bisque

A velvety roasted pumpkin bisque infused with warm spices, finished with crumbled feta, toasted pumpkin seeds, and a touch of olive oil.

Chocolate Hazelnut Mousse

Layers of rich chocolate mousse and roasted hazelnut praline, finished with silky chocolate ganache and delicate chocolate curls.

Espresso Martini

A smooth espresso martini featuring rich espresso, premium vodka, and coffee liqueur, finished with a delicate citrus twist and roasted coffee beans.
