Where It Started

Nera started as a small concept built around one color: deep violet. What began as an exploration of twilight-inspired shades quickly grew into a beauty identity centered around soft contrast, understated elegance, and a little bit of edge. The goal was to create makeup that could feel feminine without being overly sweet.


Beauty With an Edge

Nera is made for experimenting, expressing yourself, and finding confidence in the details. From everyday essentials to statement-making products, each piece is designed to complement your individual style rather than define it for you.


Our Philosophy

We believe beauty doesn't have to fit into one aesthetic: It can be soft and bold, delicate and dramatic, polished and unconventional all at once. Nera exists to make space for all of it.
